Search Engine Optimization

Technical fixes and topical authority that move rankings that actually convert. Run in-house by Lucent Growth Studio, not outsourced.

In this service

  • Full technical SEO audit
  • Keyword and topic cluster map
  • Monthly content briefs and editorial calendar
  • On-page and internal linking fixes
  • Monthly ranking and traffic reporting

Delivered by the named team on our masthead. Scope and reporting cadence are agreed in writing before any work starts.

Scope this service

Search Engine Optimization at Lucent Growth Studio starts with a full audit of what’s currently working and what isn’t — no cookie-cutter checklist, no vanity metrics. We fix technical issues that block crawling and indexing, then build topical authority around terms your actual buyers search.

From there we build a plan specific to your business: keyword clusters mapped to buying stages, internal linking that actually supports them, and content briefs written for people who convert — not for search engines.

Rankings move because the underlying page and site experience genuinely got better, which is also why the gains tend to hold. We report on the metrics that actually predict revenue, not clicks for their own sake, and we adjust the plan every month based on what the data shows.
